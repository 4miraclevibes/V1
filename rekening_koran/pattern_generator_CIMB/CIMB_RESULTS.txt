PERBEDAAN DENGAN TRSF:
======================

TRSF Pattern Generator:
- Customer name = ALL CAPS words SETELAH angka terakhir
- Bisa multiple words
- Filter bulan prefix (JAN, FEB, MAR, etc.)
- Lebih kompleks extraction logic

CIMB Pattern Generator:
- Customer name = Array[4] + Array[5] (fixed 2 words)
- Tidak perlu filter bulan
- Lebih sederhana dan konsisten
- Format lebih predictable

ALGORITHM COMPARISON:
=====================

- Find last number index
- Extract ALL CAPS words after that
- Filter month prefixes
- Result: "WILLIAM SARSONO"

CIMB: "KR OTOMATIS LLG-CIMB NIAGA CAHAYA SAKTI 0000006726 A000134 62 112923 B H550"
- Split by space
- Take array[4] and array[5]
- No filtering needed
- Result: "CAHAYA SAKTI"
