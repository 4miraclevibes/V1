TIPS TESTING
===============================================================================

1. **Copy deskripsi dari TRSF.csv** untuk real-world testing
2. **Test berbagai skenario**: perfect match, partial match, no match
3. **Bandingkan hasil** dengan BTP original di CSV
4. **Perhatikan confidence level** untuk decision making
5. **Test edge cases**: nama pendek, nama panjang, typo, dll

===============================================================================
BATCH TESTING INTERPRETATION
===============================================================================

Hasil Batch Testing akan menampilkan:

✅ MATCH       → BTP suggested = BTP original (CORRECT!)
⚠️ MISMATCH    → BTP suggested ≠ BTP original (REVIEW NEEDED)
❌ NO PATTERN  → Tidak ada pattern found (MANUAL ENTRY)

**Good Accuracy Metrics:**
- Match rate: ≥80% (8 dari 10 test match)
- Mismatch rate: ≤10% (1 dari 10 test mismatch)
- No pattern: ≤20% (2 dari 10 test no pattern)
